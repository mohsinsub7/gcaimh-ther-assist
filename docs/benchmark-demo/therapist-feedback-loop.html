2 · Where it goes — the continuous-improvement loop

  
Therapist feedback is a signal, not a verdict. The treating therapist knows the client, the tool and their own session — invaluable for finding problems, but not independent. So feedback decides where the blinded reviewers look; only blinded clinician scores become reference labels and move a release gate.

What we do not claim

      

        
    
          
  • All data shown is synthetic. There are no results yet.
    • 
          
  • Items are adapted from WAI, BLRI, MULTI-30 and CTRS-R; they are not the original validated scales.
    • 
          
  • Therapist feedback never gates a release. Safety correctness is clinician-judged under blinding, regardless of any automated score.
    • 
          
  • Feedback records are versioned and immutable; a rubric change never rewrites past scores.
